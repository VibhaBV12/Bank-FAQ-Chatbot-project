Q: What is NEFT?
A: NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) is a system to transfer money between banks electronically.

Q: What is RTGS?
A: RTGS (Real-Time Gross Settlement) allows high-value interbank money transfers in real time.

Q: What is IMPS?
A: IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) allows instant money transfers 24x7 using mobile or internet banking.

Q: How long does NEFT transfer take?
A: NEFT transfers are usually settled in batches and may take a few hours on the same working day.

Q: How long does RTGS transfer take?
A: RTGS transfers are processed in real-time, usually within minutes on working hours.

Q: How long does IMPS transfer take?
A: IMPS transfers are instant and usually complete within seconds.

Q: Can I transfer money internationally from my account?
